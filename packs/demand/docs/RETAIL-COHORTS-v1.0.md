# SYNTHETIC DEMAND & COHORT REFERENCE (`RETAIL-COHORTS-v1.0`)

**Fictional demonstration document — Lumora Wellness.**

## Section 1 (`RETAIL-v1.0-1`): Reorder and Safety-Stock Policy
Regional pharmacy distribution hubs in Kuala Lumpur and Milan maintain a 95% service-level target ($z = 1.65$) over a 10-day replenishment lead time ($L = 10$). Forecasts must be produced by statistical time-series smoothing (additive Holt-Winters backtested against a 7-day seasonal naive baseline) and never generated directly by a language model.

## Section 2 (`RETAIL-v1.0-2`): Replacement Delay Retention Policy
Customers whose replacement recovery kits are delayed by 7 or more days due to a lot hold enter the high-priority retention cohort (`COH-KL-REPL`). Any goodwill or apology offer requires positive expected net value ($\text{EV} > 0$) and explicit human approval by the Demand Planner or Customer Retention Lead before dispatch.
