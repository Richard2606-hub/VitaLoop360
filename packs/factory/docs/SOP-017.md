# SOP-017 · v3.2 · Reflow Oven Thermal Profile & Containment

## Section 4.2 · Reflow oven profile verification
`ID: SOP-017-v3.2-4.2`

SYNTHETIC PROCEDURE — fictional demonstration only. After heating-element replacement, a process engineer must verify the thermal profile against the approved recipe before the next production release. Record the profile and engineer sign-off. An energy anomaly is a hypothesis trigger, not proof of a faulty profile.

## Section 4.3 · Containment and quality release
`ID: SOP-017-v3.2-4.3`

SYNTHETIC PROCEDURE — fictional demonstration only. The supervisor may place the affected lot on hold pending profile verification and calibration review. Only a named quality reviewer may release a held lot after corrective work and evidence are recorded. No machine parameter changes are made by VitaLoop.
